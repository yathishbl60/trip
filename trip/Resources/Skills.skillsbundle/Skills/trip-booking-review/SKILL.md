# Review an existing booking

Establish the intended saved trip and itinerary day. Ask when a document could
belong to several trips or when the day is unclear. A document's arrival date
can differ from its departure date; interpret each in its own time zone.

Use Import Trip Booking with the supplied document, chosen trip, and day.
It opens Trip's review flow. Opening that flow does not mean the booking has been
saved. The person reviews the extracted facts and chooses Save in Trip.

When the person wants to enter a booking or activity manually, use Prepare a Trip
Booking or Activity with the chosen trip, day, kind, and optional title. It opens
a new draft. Ask for ambiguous days and never invent missing times. The person
reviews and saves in Trip; this does not make a reservation with any provider.

Once the person says they saved it, read the trip's itinerary to use the actual
saved records. Distinguish missing data from a cancellation and saved times from
live status. Use Edit Saved Trip Detail on the exact record if it needs correction.

Do not invent confirmation numbers, fees, baggage allowances, or missing times.
Do not claim to purchase, cancel, rebook, contact a provider, or modify a real
reservation. Trip stores the person's plan; it is not the booking provider.
