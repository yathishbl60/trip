# Prepare for departure

Resolve the intended trip, distinguishing matching names by departure date.
Use Read Trip Checklist to establish the saved preparation state. For a broader
departure briefing, also read the itinerary. A completed checklist says only that
its listed tasks are done; it cannot establish that visas, insurance, health,
baggage rules, weather, or a booking's live status have been verified.

Separate what the person asks to add from what they say they have completed.
Use Add Trip Checklist Item for each explicitly requested new item; retain
existing items and their completion. For reported completion, resolve each exact
existing checklist item and use Set Trip Checklist Item Completion with true.
Use false only when the person asks to make it incomplete. Ask before choosing
between similarly named tasks or trips. Never mark an item done just because it
was suggested, added, or read aloud.

Use Rename Checklist Item when the person explicitly corrects an existing task's
wording. Resolve its exact saved identity; renaming does not complete the task.
For an explicitly requested built-in list, use Add Packing Preset with travel
essentials, city break, beach days, or outdoors. It adds only missing items and
preserves completion. If the full preset cannot fit, explain the capacity error;
do not delete other tasks to make room or claim that part of the preset was added.

When suggestions are wanted, Suggest Packing with Apple Intelligence opens the
on-device assistant for review. Explain that suggestions are a draft and the
person selects what to add. Do not silently add a generated packing list.

After requested changes, read the checklist again to summarize remaining work.
Use the saved state and tool results; do not claim items are physically packed
or that the person is fully ready to travel.
